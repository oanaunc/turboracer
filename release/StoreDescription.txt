A garage worth saving. A road worth remembering.

Take the wheel in Afterlight: Racing Festival, an offline arcade racing adventure through a motorsport festival. Restore your family's reputation, meet four district rivals, and follow the notebook your father left behind.

TWENTY CIRCUITS
Race along the coast, through the night city, across desert roads and into the alpine district. Each destination has five circuit layouts and its own landmarks and atmosphere.

THREE WAYS TO RACE
Fight for position against three rivals, chase a time-attack target, or link drifts to build your score. Sixty campaign events reward clean driving and repeated improvement.

BUILD YOUR GARAGE
Earn credits through races, unlock six fictional cars and upgrade their performance. Choose the car that suits your line and manage your nitro through each corner.

FOLLOW THE AFTERLIGHT STORY
Collect memory chips on the road to recover notebook entries and discover the people behind the festival. Return for a daily drift challenge and track your medals and personal records.

MADE FOR OFFLINE PLAY
No account, advertisements or in-app purchases. Your progress stays on your device. Landscape touch controls include steering, braking, drifting and nitro, with adjustable steering sensitivity and optional sound, music and haptics.
