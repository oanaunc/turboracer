A garage worth saving. A festival worth winning.

Afterlight: Racing Festival is an offline arcade racer built for speed and spectacle. Restore your family's garage, beat four district champions and recover the notebook your father left behind.

TWENTY CIRCUITS, FOUR WORLDS
Race the Riviera coast, a neon night city with towering skylines, red-rock badlands and snowbound alpine passes. Every route has its own landmarks, scanned trees and rocks, animated water and weather-tinted light.

80 EVENTS, SIX WAYS TO RACE
Classic circuit races, time attack, drift runs, Elimination (last place is out every 20 seconds), Knockdown (take down three rivals) and head-to-head Duels against the district champion.

STUNTS AND TAKEDOWNS
Launch off ramps, land barrel rolls, thread near misses and slam rivals off the road under nitro. Double-tap nitro for a shockwave boost. Every stunt refills your tank.

TWELVE CARS, FIVE CLASSES
From a muscular grand-tourer to the Spectre GT3, unlock fictional cars across classes D to S with sport and race aero kits and metallic, pearl or matte paint. Upgrade each one in the Motorworks garage.

FEEL THE SPEED
Nitro flames, drift smoke, skid marks, sparks, a live engine with real gear changes, and an original soundtrack with a track for every district. Choose touch or tilt steering.

MADE FOR OFFLINE PLAY
No account, advertisements or in-app purchases. Your progress stays on your device.
